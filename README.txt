VETERAN BENEFITS NAVIGATOR — MVP
=================================

Open index.html in a modern web browser.

This is an educational prototype for organizing VA claim evidence and benefits questions.
It is NOT affiliated with VA and does not predict VA decisions or guarantee ratings.

Included:
- Claim/evidence readiness checklist
- Direct/secondary/increased/presumptive/supplemental/review paths
- Basic VA-style combined-rating calculator
- Benefits-area screening
- Official VA resource links
- Safety language against exaggerating or manufacturing evidence

Important:
- The combined-rating calculator is intentionally basic and does not apply every special rule.
- The claim score is an evidence-readiness checklist, NOT a probability of approval.
- Exact rating criteria should be verified against the current 38 CFR and current VA guidance.
